# HERO V4

## Goal

Create one of the highest quality hero sections possible.

The feeling should be:

- cinematic
- luxurious
- futuristic
- physically believable

Not gaming.

Not template.

Not cyberpunk.

------------------------------------------------

## CENTERPIECE

Create a completely custom OG emblem.

DO NOT extrude a font.

Design it as an industrial object.

Requirements

- rounded curves
- large bevels
- premium proportions
- optical glass
- brushed titanium
- subtle internal glow
- physically based rendering

It should feel like a product.

------------------------------------------------

## INTERACTION

The logo constantly follows the cursor.

Not only near screen edges.

Movement:

- quaternion rotation
- damp interpolation
- floating animation
- tiny position offset

It should feel alive.

------------------------------------------------

## SPACE

The hero exists inside deep space.

Layers

- distant galaxies
- volumetric nebula
- black hole
- asteroids
- cosmic dust
- GPU stars

Every layer moves differently.

------------------------------------------------

## LIGHTING

HDRI

Bloom

Reflection probes

Soft shadows

Rim light

Blue reflections

White highlights

------------------------------------------------

## PERFORMANCE

60 FPS.

GPU particles.

Lazy loading.

Minimal rerenders.

------------------------------------------------

Everything must feel handcrafted.
